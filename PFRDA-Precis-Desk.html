The four passage shapes and your précis order
Problem and response (PFRDA 2025): the problem and its cause, then each response in one or two sentences, then the verdict.
Concept and way forward (PFRDA 2021, 2022): what it is, why it matters, where it is used (grouped), the obstacles, the way forward.
Two views (SEBI 2020, 2025): the issue in one line, view 1, view 2, then the verdict or what it means for India.
Evolution (SEBI 2024, RBI 2022): where it started, the 2 or 3 turning points, where it is now and where it is heading.
Six ways to compress
1. A list becomes one category word. 2. Drop examples and quotes. 3. A clause becomes a phrase. 4. Keep only the number that carries the point. 5. Direct speech becomes a reported claim. 6. Merge two sentences with although, because, so or while.

Routine: 0–5 read and write gist notes, name the shape · 5–6 central theme and word budgets · 6–13 write from the notes · 13–15 title, count, proofread. Budgets follow the passage: a paragraph with a fifth of the words gets about a fifth of yours. Rubric: content 12, own words 4, structure 4, title 2, language 8; over the limit −1 (up to 10% over) or −3.
